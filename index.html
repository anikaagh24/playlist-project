Romance

The romance genre focuses on emotional relationships between characters, typically centering on love, attraction, and personal connection. Its defining feature is an emotionally satisfying ending that emphasizes hope, commitment, or lasting affection.

Dystopian

The dystopian genre portrays societies marked by oppression, control, or environmental and social collapse. These stories often follow characters who challenge the system, highlighting themes of survival, freedom, and resistance.
